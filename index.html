Boots the bundled open-source AROS ROM — no Kickstart needed. Load your own Kickstart and disk images with the controls above; everything runs locally in your browser and nothing is uploaded. Disks and ROMs mounted into the container's /files volume appear automatically in the two list dropdowns, and can also be loaded straight from the address bar, e.g. ?df0=files/game.adf or ?kick=files/kick.rom.
